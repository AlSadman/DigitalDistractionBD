Machine Learning Based Digital Behaviour Analysis System

🔬

Research Overview

This research investigates smartphone usage patterns and digital behaviour among Bangladeshi university students. A machine learning based framework was developed to analyse behavioural impact levels related to digital distraction, academic impact, social impact and focus behaviour.

🎯

Research Motivation

Increasing smartphone dependency has influenced students' learning habits, concentration ability and daily interactions. This research aims to understand these behavioural patterns and provide an intelligent assessment platform that supports awareness and healthier digital habits.

Research Objectives

📱

Objective 1

Digital Behaviour Analysis

To analyze smartphone usage patterns and digital behaviour among Bangladeshi university students.

🤖

Objective 2

Machine Learning Prediction

To apply and compare machine learning classification models for predicting behavioural impact levels.

🌐

Objective 3

Web-Based Assessment System

To develop a web application that displays Digital Distraction Level, Academic Impact Level, Social Impact Level and Focus Level with personalized recommendations.

Behavioural Prediction Dimensions

📱

Digital Distraction Level

Measures smartphone-related distraction behaviour patterns among students.

📚

Academic Impact Level

Represents the perceived influence of smartphone usage on academic activities.

🌍

Social Impact Level

Analyses the effect of digital behaviour on social interaction patterns.

🎯

Focus Level

Evaluates concentration ability and self-regulation behaviour.

💡

Research Contribution

The developed framework integrates behavioural analysis, machine learning prediction and web-based visualization to provide an accessible platform for understanding digital behaviour patterns among university students.
