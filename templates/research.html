OVERVIEW

About Our Research

This study analyses smartphone usage and digital behaviour among Bangladeshi university students and develops a machine learning-based framework for predicting digital distraction, academic impact, social impact and focus levels. The selected model was integrated into a web application to provide accessible behavioural assessment and personalized digital wellness recommendations.
