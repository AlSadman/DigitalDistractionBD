DAILY WELLNESS TOOLKIT

Healthy Digital Habits

Simple practices for maintaining physical, academic and behavioural balance during regular device use.

👁️

Screen & Eye Comfort

Avoid staring continuously at the screen for long periods. Look away regularly and allow your eyes short periods of visual rest.

Adjust screen brightness to a comfortable level.
🎯

Protect Focus Time

Use dedicated study periods where messaging, social media and entertainment applications are temporarily avoided.

One task at a time reduces unnecessary switching.
🌙

Sleep-Friendly Habits

Reduce unnecessary late-night scrolling and create a calmer transition between digital activity and sleep time.

Keep bedtime separate from endless screen browsing.
☕

Regular Digital Breaks

Break long periods of screen activity with brief movement, stretching, water or non-screen activities.

Short breaks can help reset attention.
🧍

Body & Posture Awareness

Avoid remaining in one position throughout long study sessions. Arrange your workspace comfortably and change posture regularly.

Combine screen breaks with light movement.
💬

Real-World Connection

Give meaningful offline activities and face-to-face communication regular space within your daily routine.

Digital connection should not replace every offline interaction.
